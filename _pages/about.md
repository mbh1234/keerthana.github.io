## About me

I’m drawn to problems that require both rigorous technical thinking and clear communication. At IIT Kanpur, I’ve had the chance to explore areas ranging from deep learning and embedded systems to UI/UX and competitive programming, which has shaped a broad but highly intentional research perspective.

## What I care about

- Building ideas that are technically strong and socially meaningful.
- Translating complex methods into intuitive interfaces and narratives.
- Learning through collaboration, writing, and careful iteration.
